# EXP 5: Comparative Analysis of Different Prompting Patterns

## Aim
To test and compare how different prompting techniques respond to the same task using:
- Zero-shot Prompting
- Few-shot Prompting
- Chain-of-Thought Prompting

The experiment analyzes the quality, accuracy, reasoning depth, and clarity of responses generated by AI models using different prompt structures.

---

# Scenario 1: Travel Planning

## Basic Prompt
```text
Plan a 3-day trip to Goa.
```

---

## Zero-shot Prompt

```text
Create a 3-day itinerary for Goa including beaches, food, sightseeing, and budget-friendly travel suggestions.
```

### Response Characteristics
- Gives direct itinerary
- Covers major tourist attractions
- Moderate detail level
- Minimal reasoning

### Evaluation

| Parameter | Observation |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Good |
| Clarity | Moderate |
| Detail | Medium |
| Reasoning | Low |

---

## Few-shot Prompt

### Prompt

```text
Example 1:
Input: Plan a 2-day trip to Mysore.
Output: Day 1 – Palace and Zoo visit, Day 2 – Chamundi Hills and local market. Include local food suggestions.

Example 2:
Input: Plan a 2-day trip to Ooty.
Output: Day 1 – Botanical Garden and Lake visit, Day 2 – Doddabetta Peak and tea factory. Include transport tips.

Now create a similar 3-day itinerary for Goa.
```

### Response Characteristics
- Better structure
- Consistent formatting
- Includes balanced activities
- More contextual recommendations

### Evaluation

| Parameter | Observation |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Very Good |
| Clarity | High |
| Detail | High |
| Reasoning | Moderate |

---

## Chain-of-Thought Prompt

### Prompt

```text
Plan a 3-day trip to Goa.
Think step by step:
1. Identify famous attractions.
2. Divide activities day-wise based on distance.
3. Suggest food and transport options.
4. Estimate approximate budget.
5. Provide final itinerary in organized format.
```

### Response Characteristics
- Logical planning
- Better sequencing
- More personalized suggestions
- Clear explanation behind decisions

### Evaluation

| Parameter | Observation |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Excellent |
| Clarity | Excellent |
| Detail | Very High |
| Reasoning | High |

# Comparative Analysis

| Prompting Technique | Quality | Accuracy | Reasoning Depth | Response Detail | Best Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zero-shot Prompting | Moderate | Good | Low | Medium | Simple direct tasks |
| Few-shot Prompting | High | Very Good | Moderate | High | Structured learning tasks |
| Chain-of-Thought Prompting | Excellent | Excellent | High | Very High | Complex reasoning tasks |

---

# Evaluation Method Used: Qualitative Analysis

The responses were evaluated using the following parameters:

1. **Accuracy** – Correctness and relevance of the generated response.
2. **Clarity** – Ease of understanding and organization.
3. **Detail Level** – Amount of useful information provided.
4. **Reasoning Depth** – Ability to explain logic and decision-making.

---

# Findings

- **Zero-shot prompting** works well for simple tasks but often lacks depth and reasoning.
- **Few-shot prompting** improves consistency and formatting by teaching the AI through examples.
- **Chain-of-Thought prompting** generates the most logical and detailed responses because it encourages step-by-step reasoning.

---

# Conclusion

Different prompting techniques significantly affect AI-generated responses.

- Zero-shot prompting is fast and simple.
- Few-shot prompting improves contextual understanding.
- Chain-of-Thought prompting produces the most detailed and reasoning-oriented outputs.

Thus, selecting the correct prompting strategy depends on the complexity and depth required for the task.
